## 🎙️ 3-Minute Live Winning Hackathon Pitch

### Step 1: Open Home (`/`) & Show The Command Palette
* Show the clean, Vercel/Linear-inspired floating navbar.
* Press **`Ctrl + K`** to open the Command Palette and jump to any module.

### Step 2: Test the Autonomous Voice Copilot (Groq 120B)
* Click the **Guardian Campus Copilot** orb at the bottom-right.
* Click the microphone icon or type:
  > *"I have a broken drone battery in ECE lab"*
* Watch the Copilot reason at 500 T/s and respond with the exact locker location and assign EcoCredits!

### Step 3: Run YOLO26 Vision Scanner (`/scanner`)
* Show the **explicit browser camera permission screen** (explaining why permission is needed and guaranteeing local edge privacy).
* Click "Allow Camera Access" or click the **"Lithium-Ion Battery"** chip to trigger immediate YOLO26 hazard detection and locker routing.

### Step 4: Show the Financial Valuation & Groq Salvage Inspector (`/ewaste`)
* Show the **KSPCB Bangalore Recycler & Live Metal Spot-Price Valuation Engine**:
  > *"Our campus e-waste isn't junk—it represents ₹46,666 worth of copper, gold traces, cobalt, and lithium under contract with certified recyclers in Peenya and Electronic City."*
* Click **"Run Groq 120B Salvage Feasibility Inspector"** on an Arduino board to see an instant component-level electrical engineering report!

### Step 5: Pre-Emptive Cleaning Ops (`/cleaner-ops`)
* Show the **Predictive Bin Surge Forecaster**:
  > *"Instead of reacting after bins overflow, our polynomial time-series model predicts canteen bin surges 30 minutes in advance, dispatching pre-emptive routes."*
